# Knowledge sources

Three hubs hold my context. Each has its own `AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

- `~/obsidian/obsidian-personal`: personal knowledge hub. My notes, journals,
  and life context, plus the AI memory for personal life (`.ai/data/`):
  people, documents and cases, finance, learning, reading, socials.
- `~/cockpit-core`: machine and agent ops hub. This machine, IT, VMs,
  Hetzner, AI agents and their setup, the shared skills library (Telegram,
  documents, assistants), and secrets.
- `~/obsidian/obsidian-work`: work hub. EPAM, MigVisor, and team context. Its
  skills include access to EPAM services.

cockpit-core and obsidian-work hold most integration and connection skills.
Look there before you say a tool or an access is missing.

Work-only setups, such as Grok Bot on the EPAM enterprise account, have
only `logseq-work`. They have no `cockpit-core` and no `obsidian-personal`.
Check that a hub exists before you rely on it.
